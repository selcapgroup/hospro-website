How it connects

Report once. It flows everywhere.

The value is in the hand-offs. A housekeeper taps "report a fault" on a room and the rest happens without a message being sent.

1 · Housekeeper reportsPhoto, note, priority — from the room, in HosHOUSE.
2 · Job created & assignedHosFIX job to your default fixers; push sent; room flagged.
3 · Fixer works itTimer, before/after photos, parts, receipt. Over your threshold? Manager approves in one tap.
4 · Room updates"✓ Completed by maintenance" shows on the room; reception sees it sellable.
5 · KPIs & reportResponse and fix time, cost, repeat faults — on the overview and the daily PDF.

The same pattern runs a failed fire test, a fridge out of range, a guest request, a booked event and a rate change.
