Three things worth noticing in the matrix

Operations came first

The modules in daily use are the unglamorous ones — rooms, faults, fridges, fire tests. That is where a hotel's cost and risk sit, and where adoption has to be won before anything "intelligent" is credible.

Every module produces evidence

Photos, timestamps, names and costs are captured as a by-product of doing the job. Reports and PDFs are generated, never compiled — and the same records become training data for the AI layer.

The gaps are deliberate

Live PMS feed, push, auth hardening and the intelligence layer are the items we expect a partner to accelerate. They are engineering and AI problems, not hotel problems — and the hotel problems are solved.
