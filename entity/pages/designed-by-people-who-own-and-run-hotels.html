Overview

Designed by people who own and run hotels

This is the single most important thing about HosPRO. It was not designed by a software company guessing at hotel life. It was designed, tested and rebuilt — week after week — by the people responsible for the rooms, the rota, the P&L, the fire audit and the guest reviews of a working 120-bedroom hotel and spa.

Figure 0 · Operator-designed: we run the hotel, so we feel the failures, so the software is honest

What that means for a partner

Product direction comes from the floor, not a backlog. Requirements are already validated by use. The hardest part of hospitality software — adoption by housekeepers, fixers and receptionists — is already done in two properties.

What that means for a hotel

They buy from operators who understand their day, with consultancy and training from the same people. Independents buy that way; they do not buy from a vendor's sales deck.

What we are not

Not a software company looking for a market. We are hoteliers who built the tools we needed and now want to make them a product with a serious engineering and AI partner.
