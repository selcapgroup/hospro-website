15 · The AI vision

What the intelligence looks like on the ground

Three mock-ups of how AI shows up inside existing HosPRO screens. The pattern is always the same: the system proposes, explains and waits; the person approves with one tap. Nothing "magic" — just less typing, less chasing and better decisions.

Figure 12 · Fault triage with voice · rate recommendations with confidence · a drafted morning brief

Design rules for the AI layer

  • Proposes; a named person approves. Every action is attributable.
  • Explains in one sentence a GM would say. No black boxes on the floor.
  • Works inside existing screens — no new app, no new login.
  • Degrades gracefully: if the model is down, the hotel still runs.
  • Each hotel's data trains each hotel's behaviour; nothing leaks between properties.

What makes it feasible now

  • The operating data already exists and is structured (see the database design).
  • Approvals, thresholds and roles are already in the model — agents inherit them.
  • Photos are captured on every job and room: vision models have real inputs.
  • The PMS feed closes the loop on revenue and occupancy.
  • A partner platform with permissions, agents and voice built in removes the plumbing.
