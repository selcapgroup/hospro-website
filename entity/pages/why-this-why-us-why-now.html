Why this, why us, why now

Figure B · The thesis in three panels

What we want to build with a partner

A single, multi-property, AI-driven hospitality operations and revenue platform for independent hotels — sold per property, installed in a day, and measurably paying for itself in maintenance cost, labour, rate and direct-business gains. Concretely, over the next six months:

Maintenance that costs less

AI triage and predictive maintenance on HosFIX; cost per job, first-response and fix-time KPIs; contractor-vs-in-house decisions made on data.

Rates that beat the comp-set

HosREV with live rate-shopper and PMS feeds, event detection, forecasting, explanations and guard-railed auto-approval — and the one thing no RMS has: knowledge of what is happening in the building.

More venue and events business

Minutes-not-days enquiry response with an AI agent, instant configurator quotes, pipeline discipline, and campaigns to a guest database the hotel actually owns.

Guests who feel it

Faults fixed before they are found, requests routed and tracked, a concierge that answers at 2 a.m., and a post-stay loop that brings people back direct.

The sequence matters: operations first (adoption and data), revenue second (margin), growth third (top line), intelligence throughout. That is the order a hotel feels the benefit — and the order the roadmap in the Partnership tab follows.

What we are not trying to be

We are not building a PMS, a channel manager or a booking engine; those exist and integrate well. We are not building a generic chatbot. We are building the operating layer that sits around the PMS and makes every department, and eventually every rate decision, measurably better — then licensing it to the thousands of hotels that look like ours.
