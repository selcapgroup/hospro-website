HosTIME & HosME: time, attendance and the staff's own app

Labour is the biggest controllable cost in a hotel and the least measured: rotas are drawn, people turn up roughly when planned, and payroll is run on the rota. HosTIME replaces the guess with clocked reality; HosME gives every member of staff their own app for leave, profile and (next) their shifts. Both went live at Brandon Hall in the week of 17 November 2026 for 52 staff.

Figure P · Rota → approval → clock-in → attendance → payroll, with HosME closing the loop to the member of staff

Built

  • QR clock-in by department, PIN, optional location check
  • Live board: rota vs actual, lateness, hours, on-site now
  • Rota approval with inline edits and coverage overview
  • HosME: profile, holiday, leave requests, alerts, auto-login link
  • Payroll: rota vs clocked hours, adjustments, net pay by period

What we want next

  • Shifts and swaps in HosME; shift confirmations
  • Pay on actual clocked hours (decision pending a clean week)
  • Labour vs occupancy and vs covers, by hour
  • Agency and overtime tracking against budget

HosPRO·AIwith the JV

  • Rota suggestions from the occupancy and covers forecast
  • Lateness and no-show patterns flagged early
  • Fair-load and skills-aware allocation across departments
  • Labour cost forecast per day, per department, before the week starts
