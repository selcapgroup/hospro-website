Compliance & training: fire, kitchen and housekeeping in one register

HosFIRE began as a fire-safety record. It has become the hotel's compliance spine: every check, every course, every certificate for fire, kitchen food safety and housekeeping COSHH, with the paper forms kept word-for-word so staff and inspectors recognise them.

Fire (HosFIRE)

  • Weekly alarm tests, drills, activations with call-point and result
  • Four marshals, training matrix, policies, diary of actions
  • HosFIRE Mobile for the marshal on site (photos, progress, documents)
  • Inspector PDF pack, always current
  • Failed test → HosFIX job automatically

Kitchen (HosKitchen)

  • The head chef's seven paper forms digitised, same wording, references BHK-01 to 07: opening and closing checks, temperatures, daily and weekly cleaning, staff food log, wastage
  • No login — staff code, name, time and finger signature; QR label per form
  • Out-of-range readings (fridge >5 °C, freezer >−18 °C, core <75 °C, deliveries) require a corrective action before saving
  • Manager sign-off; 120-day history; printable day sheets

Housekeeping

  • Combined course: COSHH, hygiene and room standards — 14 sections, 20-question check, pass mark 16, certificate, 12-month renewal
  • Colour-coded cloths and the product register from the hotel's own supplies
  • Policy BHH-HK-01 v2, step guide and one-page recap with QR to the staff page
  • Training register shows who is current, who lapses when
Figure Q · One compliance spine — courses, checks, register and pack — and where the AI guardian sits

All three use the same pattern: a shareable staff page with a simple code (no accounts to manage), create-only records (nothing can be quietly edited), and manager sign-off through the logged-in portal. The kitchen course is not a substitute for an accredited food-hygiene certificate and says so.
