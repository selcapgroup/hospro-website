HosF&B: the kitchen, the bar and the restaurant as one P&L

Food and beverage is the second-largest revenue line in most independent hotels and the least understood. The chef knows what a dish costs in his head; the accounts show a food cost months later; nobody knows the profit on tonight's service. HosF&B closes that gap — from the chef's costing sheet to the waiter's order pad to the Xero invoice.

Status: built as a standalone tool in November 2026 (brandon.hospro.co.uk/hosfb), tested end-to-end against a stand-in database; live Firebase rules, Xero connection and the first real service are the next steps. Tagged PARTIAL throughout.

Figure O · HosF&B — plan and cost → sell and serve → count and account, in one set of numbers

What exists (Nov 2026)

  • Kitchen: ingredients, dishes, menus, P&L per menu and per dish, chef's estimates vs actuals
  • Events costing; bar stock, stock take and supplier orders; daily P&L; invoices; Xero screen
  • Guest booking page with pre-order; order pad; kitchen screen; till import
  • All sums unit-tested; full booking → seat → order → kitchen → close → P&L flow tested on tablet and phone
  • Brandon Hall branded; cloud functions written for invoice reading and Xero

What we want next

  • Live Xero link (bills → price checks, sales → accounts) and month-end stock counts feeding the accounts
  • Bar sales and bar P&L from till data; par levels and suppliers on the new bar list
  • Cloud printing for kitchen tickets; guest confirmation emails
  • HosChef phone app: scan a product label to pull ingredient data and allergens straight from packaging
  • Link into the one all-in-one app (app.html)

HosPRO·AIwith the JV

  • Dish-level demand forecast → prep lists and purchasing
  • Waste and variance anomalies flagged automatically
  • Menu engineering: which dishes to promote, re-price or drop
  • Invoice reading at scale (every supplier, every line)
  • Allergen and label recognition from a photo
  • Event quotes drafted from the costing engine

Why it matters to a hotel — and to a partner

The costs dashboard at Brandon Hall found that, March to September, the accounts charged £26,541 less in food and beverage cost of sales than Xero showed purchased — because nobody counts stock at month-end and covers are under-recorded. That is not an accounting problem; it is an operating-data problem, and HosF&B is the operating data. For a partner, it is a second margin engine beside HosREV, in a part of the hotel where no PMS, RMS or till ever goes.
