15 · The AI vision

How we will develop the models: only ever to enhance the system

Every model follows the same loop, and none of them goes live without first running silently beside the people it is meant to help. The loop is built from parts the platform already has — events, statuses, approvals — so a model is just another contributor whose proposals are measured like everyone else's.

Figure M · The eight-step model loop — shadow first, assist second, autonomy last and only inside guard-rails

Why per-property models

A 120-room spa hotel and a 70-room inn have different rhythms, assets and guests. Models trained per hotel are more accurate and keep each hotel's data its own; only anonymised benchmarks are shared across the estate.

What the partner platform provides

Training and serving infrastructure, model registry and versioning, evaluation, prompt and agent tooling, voice, and — critically — permissions, approvals and audit built in so the loop above is enforced by the platform, not by good intentions.

What HosPRO provides

The event stream, the labels (every outcome is recorded), the workflows the models plug into, the operators who judge the proposals, and two hotels to run shadow mode in from month one.
