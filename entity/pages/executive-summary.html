Executive summary

HosPRO is a hotel operating platform we are developing inside live hotels, designed by people who own and run hotels. It replaces WhatsApp, printouts and seven disconnected systems with one connected layer that every department uses on their phone — and gives the general manager one live picture of the whole property.

The proposition in four lines

Built, not slideware. Housekeeping, maintenance, reception, F&B, fire safety, sales, rota, marketing, P&L and a revenue-management module are live and in daily use.

Operations-first. Every feature exists because a housekeeper, fixer or receptionist needed it on a Tuesday morning. Nothing exists because a vendor roadmap said so.

Measured. Photo proof, timers, costs, approvals and completion loops produce real operating data — the raw material for AI.

Ready for a partner. We bring the hotel domain, the live customer base and the data model. We are looking for a technology partner to take the AI, agent and voice layer to product grade and scale it across the independent-hotel market.

"Common sense first, technology second. The system has to make the person on the floor faster, not slower — or it is not a system, it is admin."
