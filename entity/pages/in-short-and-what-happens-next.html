In short — and what happens next

HosPRO is the rare thing in hospitality technology: a connected operating platform that already runs real hotels, built on common sense by the people who run them. Add a serious AI and engineering partner and it becomes the operating system for the independent-hotel market.

Why now

  • The hard part — adoption on the floor — is done and proven in two properties.
  • The data a model needs is already being captured every hour of every day.
  • PMS access (Guestline) is agreed; rate and channel feeds are next.
  • Independent hotels are under margin pressure and ready for tools priced for them.
  • Agent and voice technology has matured to the point where "approve with one tap" is realistic, not a demo.

Proposed first conversation

  1. Walk-through of the live platform at Brandon Hall (on site or screen-share) and a look at the data model.
  2. Partner review of the codebase and the Ada Engine fit; agree the months 1–2 scope (fault triage, allocation, morning brief, auth).
  3. Outline JV terms: roles, IP in the intelligence layer, revenue share per property, governance.
  4. Build-and-measure at Brandon Hall and The Barn for 90 days against the business-case model.
  5. Decide, publish the case studies and open the pipeline.
