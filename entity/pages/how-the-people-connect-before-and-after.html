How the people connect: before and after

A hotel is a network of people, not a hierarchy. Today that network runs on calls, WhatsApp and walking the corridor. With HosPRO every person keeps their role — the system becomes the hub they all talk through, so a hand-off is a record, not a conversation that has to be remembered.
