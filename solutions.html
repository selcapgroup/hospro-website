Six ways HOSPRO makes a hotel run better and earn more.

Hotels tell us the same three things within five minutes: maintenance costs too much and takes too long, rates are set blind against the competition, and there is never enough business through the door. We built the platform around those — then the rest of the hotel followed.

Maintenance that costs less

The fastest money a hotel can save. A lost fault typically costs a goodwill gesture, a contractor call-out and three nights of a room out of order; the same fault in HosFIX costs a fixer's time and £12 of parts — and the record stops it happening again.

  • Faults never lost; first response and fix time measured
  • Spend approved before it is spent; receipts at source
  • Repeat faults by room and asset; contractor vs in-house decisions on data

More events and direct business

Speed wins venue enquiries. HosEVENTS puts every enquiry in a pipeline with owners and follow-ups, builds quotes from your mapped venues and packages, and feeds booked events to the kitchen, the rooms and maintenance. HosMARKETING turns Wi-Fi sign-ups into a guest database you own.

  • Enquiry to signed agreement in one place

The chef costs dishes and menus with profit per dish; actual prices arrive from invoices; sales come from the till or the order pad; labour comes from the rota. The result is a daily F&B P&L — and month-end stock counts that make the accounts true.

  • Bar stock take on a phone, variance at cost
  • Supplier price checks and anomalies
