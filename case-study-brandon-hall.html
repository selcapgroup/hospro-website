Client story · Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa

A whole hotel, running on one platform.

Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa is a 120-bedroom country house hotel in 17 acres of Warwickshire gardens and woodland, with a spa, restaurant and 14 spaces for meetings, weddings and celebrations. It is where HOSPRO was designed, tested and rebuilt — by the people who run it.

The challenge

Plenty of demand, too many places to keep track of it.

Enquiries arrived from every direction and were quoted by hand. Maintenance ran on WhatsApp, so faults were lost and rooms sold broken. Housekeeping worked from a printout. Fire checks lived on a clipboard, kitchen checks on paper forms. Payroll was run on the rota, not on hours worked. The owners saw the month, never the day.

The approach

Operations first, then everything else.

We started on the floor. Housekeeping moved onto phones with photo checklists; faults began flowing into HosFIX with timers, photos and a £50 approval gate; reception got a wall board for requests, deliveries and incidents. Only when those were in daily use did we add fire and kitchen compliance, time and attendance for 52 staff, the events pipeline, the Wi-Fi guest database, F&B costing and the dashboards — each one sharing the same database.

  • Every function room measured, capacities per layout, technology audited
  • The head chef's seven paper forms digitised word-for-word
  • Posters and personal links; the app installed from a link in under a minute
What changed

Measured, not guessed.

  • Room times, first-response and fix times, cost per job — captured as a by-product of the work
  • No WhatsApp group for maintenance; completed jobs show on the room in housekeeping
  • Around 80 consented guest records a day from the Wi-Fi from day one
  • Fire, kitchen and housekeeping records inspector-ready at any moment
  • Rota vs clocked hours reconciled before payroll
  • The cost dashboard surfaced over-priced kegs, overlapping wholesalers, a duplicate posting and a £26k stock-adjustment gap in its first week
  • A one-page Daily Operations Report for the owners every night, compiled by nobody
"Common sense first, technology second. The system has to make the person on the floor faster, not slower — or it is not a system, it is admin."

The HOSPRO operating principle, written at Brandon Hall.
