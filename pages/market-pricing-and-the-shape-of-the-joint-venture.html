Market, pricing and the shape of the joint venture

Market

Thousands of UK independent hotels, inns, spa hotels and wedding venues sit between 20 and 200 rooms, most running a PMS plus WhatsApp. The buyer's guide segments them as boutiques and independents (20–100 rooms, high-touch, medium complexity) and mid-size properties with departmental silos — precisely where HosPRO is already proven. Groups of three to twenty properties and hotel consultancies are a natural channel, and the model travels to Europe and the Gulf, where the same owner-operator pattern dominates.

Pricing model (proposed)

Per-property subscription by room band with the operating modules bundled; an intelligence tier (HosREV autopilot, agents, concierge) on top; onboarding and consultancy delivered by HosPRO. Comparable AI pricing tools alone charge independents a per-property subscription and show double-digit revenue uplifts; HosPRO bundles that with the operations layer. A 120-room hotel paying a few hundred pounds a month against a six-figure annual benefit is an easy conversation.

Unit economics to prove in the pilot

  • Onboarding cost per property (target: one day, one person)
  • Hosting cost per property (today: tens of pounds a month)
  • Measured benefit per property across the seven levers
  • Payback period (target: under three months)

What we are asking of a partner

  • Engineering ownership of the platform layer: auth, multi-tenancy, deployment, observability.
  • AI and agent capability: models, forecasting, triage, voice, with human approval built in.
  • A joint product roadmap and shared IP in the intelligence layer; HosPRO retains the hospitality domain, brand and customer relationships.
  • A commercial structure that rewards both parties on recurring revenue per property.

What we commit

  • Two live properties as build-and-measure sites from day one.
  • Full access to the current codebase, data model and operating workflows.
  • Product, domain and go-to-market leadership; onboarding and training delivery.
  • A pipeline of independent hotels through our consultancy and group relationships.

Governance

Monthly steering (product, engineering, commercial); gates at months 2, 4 and 6 with pre-agreed measures; decisions on IP, pricing and channel taken jointly; hotel data remains each hotel's, under HosPRO's existing privacy terms.
