Why it compounds

Each module makes the next cheaper: housekeeping already knows the rooms, maintenance already knows the fixers, reception already knows today's arrivals. By the tenth module the marginal cost of a new department is a configuration file. And every month of operation adds data that makes the AI layer more accurate for that hotel.
