15 · The AI vision

Where we are: an honest starting point

HosPRO contains no AI today. Every decision in the system is made by a person; every automation is a rule we wrote (a failed fire test raises a job, a fridge out of range raises a job, spend over £50 waits for approval). That is deliberate — the operating system had to work and be trusted before anything intelligent sat on top of it. What exists now is the part most AI projects skip: the clean, structured, evidenced operating data.
