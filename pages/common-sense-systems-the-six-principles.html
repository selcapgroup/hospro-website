4 · Our approach

Common-sense systems: the six principles

HosPRO was not designed in a workshop. It was built week by week inside Brandon Hall Hotel & Spa — a 120-bedroom country-house hotel with a spa, restaurant, meetings and weddings — by the people running it. Every principle below is a lesson paid for on the floor.

Figure 2 · The six operating principles every HosPRO module is tested against

What "common sense" means in practice

  • If a feature takes more taps than WhatsApp, it loses — so it is redesigned until it wins.
  • Data is captured as a by-product of the work (a completion photo), never as a separate task ("fill in the log").
  • The desktop is for managers and reports; the floor runs on phones.
  • Thresholds, defaults and assignments are configured per hotel in minutes, not coded per client.
  • Each hotel keeps its own data in its own collections; the platform is shared.

Where this comes from

HosPRO is run by hotel operators and commercial leaders, not a software vendor. The same team manages Brandon Hall's commercial plan, its P&L, its fire audit preparation and its brand — so the software is shaped by owning the outcome, not by selling licences.

That is the asset we bring to a JV: the domain, the live customers, the data model and the operating know-how. What we want from a partner is the engineering platform, AI and agent capability to turn it into a product at scale.
