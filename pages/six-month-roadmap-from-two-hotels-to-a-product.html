Six-month roadmap: from two hotels to a product

Six months, three gates. Month 1–2 proves the partner platform on live operations; months 3–4 ship the revenue and sales intelligence that hotels pay most for; months 5–6 make it a product any independent can switch on. Every month ends with something measurable in a real hotel.

Interactive: what is live at the end of each month

Figure I · Six tracks, three gates — every month ends with something live in a real hotel

Months 1–2 · Prove

Partner platform under the live HosFIX and HosHOUSE modules; real auth; KPI baselines captured; first AI features (triage, allocation, brief) in daily use at two hotels. Success = fixer and housekeeper adoption unchanged, response times down.

Months 3–4 · Earn

HosREV on live feeds with forecasting and explanations; AI enquiry agent answering in minutes; concierge by message. Success = measured RevPAR, enquiry response time and conversion against the baseline; three further independents piloting.

Months 5–6 · Scale

Multi-tenant onboarding in a day; guard-railed pricing autopilot; parts, labour and voice features; pricing and channel agreed; ten hotels signed. Success = a product a consultancy or group can roll out without us in the room.
