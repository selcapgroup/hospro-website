14 · Guest data & marketing

The marketing engine: turning Wi-Fi into a guest database

Hotels have thousands of people walk through their doors who never become a contact. HosPRO's captive portal captures them with consent in the thirty seconds it takes to join the Wi-Fi — around 80 records a day at Brandon Hall from the first day live — and gives the hotel a database, a map and a newsletter tool it owns.

Figure 10 · From hotspot to newsletter in five steps — the hotel owns every record

Privacy by design

Consent is explicit and unticked by default; "18 or over" is part of the marketing opt-in; terms link to the hotel's privacy policy; a guest record can be edited, unsubscribed or deleted in one click across all their visits; exports respect the filters. The hotel is the data controller and the data sits in its own project.

Why this matters to a JV partner

Guest data plus operational data in one place is rare below chain level. It makes genuinely useful AI possible: a guest who came for the spa twice, books Saturdays, lives 30 miles away and once reported a cold room is a sentence the system can already write — and act on.
