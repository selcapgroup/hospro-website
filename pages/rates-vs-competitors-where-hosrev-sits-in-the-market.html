What the market proves

Independents will pay a per-property subscription for pricing automation, and they get double-digit uplifts when they do. RMS is no longer exotic — it is a line item a 70-room hotel expects.

Our edge

Every RMS prices the hotel it thinks exists. HosREV prices the hotel that exists today: two suites out of order, a 60-pax conference holding eight rooms, a wedding closing the restaurant. Operational truth is a pricing input nobody else has.

JV scope

Partner-grade forecasting, event detection and explanations; rate-shopper and channel connectors; guard-railed autopilot; group displacement and events pricing; a benchmark across the HosPRO estate. Optionally, integrate with an existing RMS as the operational feed rather than compete head-on.
