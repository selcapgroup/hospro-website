17 · The joint venture

Target architecture: HosPRO + Ada Engine

Immediate Impact's Ada Engine describes itself as a foundation for designing, building, connecting and deploying custom AI-powered applications — combining interfaces, business logic, models, company knowledge, existing systems, permissions, human approvals, agentic actions and voice. That is almost exactly the layer HosPRO needs next. The fit is complementary: we bring the domain, the data model, the live properties and the operating know-how; the partner brings the engineering platform and AI capability to make it product-grade and scalable.

Months 1–2 · Prove

Re-platform the HosFIX and HosHOUSE modules on the partner foundation with real auth and roles; ship fault triage, smart allocation and the drafted morning brief at Brandon Hall and The Barn. Measure against the business-case model.

Months 3–4 · Earn

HosREV on live feeds with forecasting and explanations; AI enquiry agent and instant quotes; guest concierge by message; compliance guardian. Three pilot independents live alongside the two reference hotels.

Months 5–6 · Scale

Multi-tenant onboarding in a day; guard-railed pricing autopilot and channel push; voice concierge; partner channel through consultancies and groups; ten hotels signed at launch.
