11 · Maintenance efficiency

Maintenance: the fastest money a hotel can save

Maintenance is where independent hotels bleed quietly: rooms out of order that could be selling, contractors called for jobs the team could do, parts bought twice, and no idea what anything costs until the invoice. HosFIX already closes most of these gaps; the AI layer closes the rest.

What AI adds (JV scope)

  • Triage from a photo and a sentence — work type, priority, likely parts, the right fixer, and whether the room is sold tonight.
  • Predictive maintenance — recurring faults per room and asset (boilers, showers, locks, fridges) scheduled before they fail; seasonal patterns learned per property.
  • Contractor decision support — "this is a £12 in-house job" vs "call the gas engineer"; approval requests drafted with the evidence attached.
  • Parts and stock — usage per job type; reorder prompts; cost benchmarks across the estate.
  • Voice on the job — fixers log materials, notes and completion hands-free.
  • Rate link — rooms out of order feed HosREV so inventory and pricing reflect reality.

Target outcomes (per 120-room hotel)

Urgent first response under 1 hour · average fix time −30% · out-of-order nights −50% · contractor spend −25% · every job costed. Measured in the 90-day build-and-measure.
