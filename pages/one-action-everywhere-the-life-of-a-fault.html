Other automatic flows already live

  • Failed fire-alarm test or activation → urgent HosFIX job
  • Fridge temperature out of range → high-priority job
  • Reception "housekeeping issue" → today's room flagged urgent in HosHOUSE
  • Daily F&B figures → desktop tracker and manager overview

What the manager sees

  • Needs-attention list: urgent open jobs, blocked rooms, overdue follow-ups
  • Rooms cleaned vs planned, raised vs fixed, open by area
  • Rooms 360°: everything that ever happened to one room

Why this is the AI foundation

Every arrow in Figure 4 writes a timestamped, attributed, photographed record. Within months a hotel holds thousands of structured events: who, where, what, how long, how much. That is exactly the data an AI layer needs — and exactly what no hotel has today.
