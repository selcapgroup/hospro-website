3 · The problem

How an independent hotel actually runs today

A 120-room hotel with a spa, restaurant and events runs on a patchwork: a property-management system for rooms, a printout for housekeeping, WhatsApp for maintenance, a clipboard for fire checks, spreadsheets for F&B figures, email for sales enquiries and a monthly accountant's pack for the numbers. None of them talk to each other. The glue is people — and the glue breaks every day.

Figure 1 · Six systems, zero connections — the daily reality in most independent hotels

Why the big suites don't fix it

Enterprise hotel software is priced and designed for chains: long implementations, per-module licences, and workflows the hotel must bend to. Independents end up using 20% of it and still run WhatsApp on the side.

Why point apps don't fix it

A maintenance app here, a housekeeping app there — each with its own login, its own data and no idea the other exists. The hand-off between departments is exactly where the value is, and exactly what point apps miss.

What the opportunity is

One connected operating layer, built around how a hotel actually works, cheap enough for a 70-room property, installable on any phone in a day — and generating the operational data that makes AI genuinely useful.
