12 · Revenue management

HosREV: rate recommendations a GM can trust

Most independent hotels price by instinct or copy the neighbour. HosREV gives them a revenue manager in the app: every day it looks at pick-up, the competitive set within 20 miles, local events and seasonality and proposes a rate per room type per night. Phase 1 is deliberately recommend and approve — nothing changes in the PMS without a human tap. That is common sense, and it is how trust is built before automation.

Recommendation engine on demo and hotel-supplied data · room-type ladder · comp-set and events calendar · ADR/RevPAR tracking · approval workflow · access-gated for the revenue lead.
