🧯 HosFIRE LIVE

Fire, kitchen & housekeeping compliance.

One compliance register for the whole hotel: fire safety, kitchen food safety and housekeeping COSHH — with the inspector pack always ready.

Who uses it: Fire marshals · head chef · housekeeping lead · general manager · inspectors

✅

What it does today

  • Weekly alarm tests, drills and activations with call-point and result; marshals, training matrix, policies, actions diary
  • HosFIRE Mobile for the marshal on site: progress, notes, photos, documents
  • HosKitchen: the head chef's daily forms digitised word-for-word with QR labels — opening/closing checks, temperatures, cleaning, staff food, wastage; readings outside limits need a corrective action
  • Housekeeping: combined COSHH, hygiene and room-standards course with certificate and 12-month renewal
  • Online courses with checks and certificates; training register showing who is current
  • Create-only records; manager sign-off; inspector PDF pack
🔭

What is coming

  • AI compliance guardian: watches readings and renewal dates, drafts corrective actions, assembles the pack

Part of one system

HosFIRE shares its database with every other HOSPRO module, so what happens here shows up where it matters — on the room, on the overview, in the daily report and in the numbers.
